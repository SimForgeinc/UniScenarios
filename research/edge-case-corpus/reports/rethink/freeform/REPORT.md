## 2. Harness (freedom arm)

`tools/research/freeform/harness.py`. Per brief, zero per-brief tuning:

1. `author` — one LLM call; the model emits `{ambient, ambientSettleS, structureNote,
   template}` where `template` is a complete ScenarioTemplateV2 (full vocabulary:
   up to 64 actors, interactions/triggers on any actor pair, signal phase programs and
   `set signal:*.phase`, `laneOffset` swerves, `changeLane`, ambient presets, weather).
   Context: `VOCAB.md` (schema digest + verified MAP/PHYSICS/GATE facts + structure
   inventory) + 2 gold templates (shape reference).
2. Unified repair loop, budget **3** repair/revise calls total (RETHINK-PLAN §3A
   "2–3 repair rounds"): `template validate` errors verbatim → repair; zero sites on
   every map → site repair with per-map matcher failureSummary; probe batch
   (draws=4, max-sites=4, all ready maps) → frozen tg_gate census per criterion +
   refusal codes + raw-trace facts → revise. A repair that breaks validation at budget
   exhaustion falls back to the last valid+sited template (recorded).
3. Final measured batch: **draws=10, max-sites=10, all ready maps** — exactly the
   frozen `author_llm.py` defaults, so the baseline arm is like-for-like.
4. Frozen gate v2 on raw traces + portability (≥2 maps, ≥3 sites) decide admission;
   dynamism census on every feasible final cell; up to **6 cells/brief** exported to
   the rethink cell-artifact contract (`cells/<cellId>/{instance.json, trace.json.gz,
   meta.json, census.json}`, passing cells first, round-robin across maps); batch dirs
   deleted after extraction.

Portability rules (lane contract, checked pre-engine): no `scene_absolute` roles, no
`anchor.pin`, no map-bound route modes; a role literally named `ego`.

Baseline arm: `tools/research/freeform/baseline_arm.py` drives the **frozen, unmodified**
`tools/gates/author_llm.py` (`author_brief`) over the same sample with its own frozen
protocol (probe draws=4/max-sites=6, final draws=10/max-sites=10, luna/medium — the
published W7 config). Two outside seams, neither visible to the pipeline: batch outdirs
redirected into the run dir for extraction/cleanup; `vlm.ask` replaced by a
byte-equivalent client that also records token usage (the original discards it).
Baseline cells are exported to the same cell contract (arm=`baseline`).

## 3. Pre-registration (written BEFORE any measured arm; grid subset frozen in
`tools/research/freeform/grid-subset.json`)

**Model/effort grid (owner directive):** {gpt-5.6-luna, sol, terra} × {low, medium,
high} on a fixed 15-brief subset of the frozen sample (9 DEV stratified by category +
6 owner stratified by group, seed 20260817). Selection rule, declared before looking:

1. higher admitted count on the subset;
2. tie → higher mean `interactingPairs` over gate-passing cells (shared census);
3. tie → lower total tokens.

The winner authors the full 50-brief frozen sample. Per-arm tokens and wall time are
recorded from the run reports (`usageTotal`, `wallSeconds`).

**Effort hypothesis (stated before running):** W8 (compiler surface, merged 2026-08-16)
falsified "effort raises admission" — flat-to-down across 30 arms while reasoning
tokens rose 17–30×. That surface gave the model ~10 scalar fields to decide. On THIS
surface the model authors the entire template, so if reasoning effort matters anywhere
in authoring, it must show here: **H-freedom-effort: within each model, admission on
the grid subset is non-decreasing low→medium→high, with a strictly positive
low→high delta for at least two of the three models.** Flat-or-down replicates W8's
negative on a surface with real freedom and makes "effort buys nothing in authoring"
a two-surface finding. Secondary, same direction: census `interactingPairs` (passing
cells) and validity-after-repairs.

**Falsifiers (RETHINK-PLAN §3A, restated):**
- template validity < 50% after repairs → vocabulary too hard to wield blind;
- gate floor: admission < 0.35 of briefs → freedom arm misses the usable floor;
- dynamism census not better than the compiler arm → freedom didn't buy dynamism;
- footage realism (FootageLane's judge) worse than compiler arm → freedom hurt realism.
Any of these, measured honestly, kills or reshapes the stream.

**Authoring-surface freeze:** sha256 over (VOCAB.md, harness.py, llm.py,
shared/dynamism_census.py) is printed by every run (`surfaceSha256`) and recorded here
before the main arms; pilot may still change the surface, grid/main runs may not.
